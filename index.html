RegimeMy CTC is ₹14L and I claim ₹2.5L in 80C+NPS+80D. Which regime saves more?
In FY 2025-26 the new regime gives you ₹75,000 standard deduction and tighter slabs, while the old regime lets you claim 80C/80D/HRA. At ₹14L gross with ₹2.5L of deductions plus a likely HRA, the old regime is usually marginally cheaper by ₹6–18k — but it flips against you if you live in your own house or under-utilise 80C. Run our Old vs New calculator with your actual HRA and it'll show the rupee gap.
CA Ashish Sharma · Updated for FY 2025-26
GSTI run a kirana doing ₹65L turnover. Composition or Regular GST?
At ₹65L turnover you're eligible for Composition (1% for traders, threshold ₹1.5Cr). Composition wins if your customers don't need GST invoices and your inward ITC is small — most kiranas fit this. You lose if you sell B2B or buy a lot of taxable inputs. Use our Composition vs Regular calculator to see the net-outgo gap.
CA Ashish Sharma · 8 May 2026
MSMECan a 2-yr-old printing press in Jaipur get CGTMSE-backed loan?
Yes. CGTMSE covers MSME units (Udyam-registered) for collateral-free loans up to ₹5Cr. A 2-year vintage with clean GST returns is well within eligibility — the bank needs Udyam, 3 yrs ITRs (or audited financials), GST returns and a project report. Try our MSME Loan Eligibility checker — it also flags Mudra and PMEGP if you qualify there too.
CA Ashish Sharma · MSME desk
HRAI pay rent to my mother — is the HRA exemption actually allowed?
Yes, fully allowed — but the rent must be real. Keep a rent agreement, transfer via bank (not cash) and your mother declares the rental income in her ITR. If she's a senior citizen with no other income, her tax on that rent can be zero. The exemption isn't disallowed just because the landlord is family — it's disallowed if you can't prove it.
CA Ashish Sharma · Salaried desk
Capital gainsSold equity MF after 14 months. STCG or LTCG, and how much tax?
Holding period for equity MF is 12 months for LTCG. At 14 months you're in LTCG territory — taxed at 12.5% (post Jul 2024 amendment) on gains above ₹1.25L per year. So if your gain is ₹2L, tax is 12.5% × ₹75k = ₹9,375 + cess.
CA Ashish Sharma · Investor desk
TDSMy employer deducts TDS on my full salary even though I have heavy 80C — can I fix this?
Yes — submit a fresh Form 12BB with your investment proofs to HR. They'll recompute TDS for the rest of the year and the deducted excess gets adjusted against future months. If the year ends with excess TDS, claim refund in your ITR.
CA Ashish Sharma · Salaried desk
